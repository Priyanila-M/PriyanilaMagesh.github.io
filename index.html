About

Immunology scientist with expertise in immunoengineering, drug screening, and DNA manipulation workflows. Proficient in T cell biology, gene editing, multicolor flow cytometry, in vivo pharmacology, and mechanistic immune profiling in complex disease models.

Skilled in integrating experimental immunology with transcriptomics data to build next‑generation CAR T therapies, with multiple industry R&D internships collaborating across cross‑functional teams to drive product development.

Currently a PhD Candidate at the University of Minnesota, researching immune mechanobiology, onco‑immunology, and genomics.

Core Skills

Immunology

CAR T cell engineering, immunophenotyping, cytokine profiling, ELISA, ELISpot, cytotoxicity assays, flow cytometry (16+ colors), cell sorting, target inhibition studies.

Molecular Biology & Virology

CRISPR‑Cas9, sgRNA design, molecular cloning, bacterial transformation, transfection, transduction, RNA extraction, RT‑qPCR, protein purification, Western blotting.

Model Systems

Primary T cell isolation and culture, 3D culture (spheroids and organoids), organotypic live tumor slice culture, biomaterial‑based systems.

In Vivo Pharmacology

Orthotopic tumor grafting, intraperitoneal injection, submandibular blood collection, necropsy, IHC, tissue sectioning, IVIS imaging, small animal MRI.

Imaging

Two‑photon microscopy, confocal microscopy, FLIM, light sheet microscopy.

Computational Analysis

Python, R, MATLAB, FlowJo, FACSDiva, ImageJ, SnapGene, AutoDock, PyMOL, GraphPad Prism.
